## 1. Delivery framework

The programme will run with SAFe. Program Increments last 10 weeks with 2-week sprints. PI-1 starts on 14 September 2026.

**Decision:** Jira remains the master tool for the backlog. Any framework or tool used by the consulting team must synchronise with the client's Jira; no parallel backlog is allowed.

## 2. Vocabulary

Claire Martin explained that Acme teams say "Plant BOM" for the list of operations and stations used to build the e-axle. The consulting team uses PBOM, and 3DEXPERIENCE calls it BOP. All three names will be treated as the same concept.

Documents will be written in French or English depending on the site. Lyon writes mostly in French, Sevilla in English.

## 3. Scope

In scope: CAD to PLM integration, PLM to ERP part master and BOM publication, engineering change propagation, Process BOM publication to MES.
